# Mathematical and Computational Biology In Drug Discovery (University of Basel)

See course information here: https://accio.github.io/MCBDD/

This is the follow-up to https://accio.github.io/AMIDD/

## Topics

From biology:
- Genetics and population genetics,
- Functional genomics,
- Transcriptomics and proteomics,
- Chemical biology,
- Antisense oligonucleotides and antibodies,
- Pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics,

From mathematics/computer science:
- Reproducible research tools: git, Snakemake/NextFlow, conda, etc...,
- Linear models and generalized linear models,
- Inference methods, such as the Expectation-Maximization (EM) algorithm, Hidden Markov Models (HHMs), clustering algorithms, Monte-Carlo methods, and variational methods,
- Generative models,
- Gene network inference,
- Deep-neural networks,
- Machine learning, deep learning and Gaussian Processes,
- Mathematical modelling,

## Syllabus

### Module 0: Introduction to mathematical and computational biology in drug discovery

### Module 1: What are drug targets and where to find them?

### Module 2: What can we do if there are no good targets?
Discuss alternatives to target-based drug discovery. In particular, phenotypic drug discovery. Covers two parts:

1. Phenotypic screening with genetic and chemogenomic libraries,
2. Molecular phenotypic screening based on gene expression.

### Module 3: What kind of drug should we develop?
Modality selection from a computational point of view. The goal is to introduce essentials of drug modalities, in particular emerging modalities such as small-molecule splicing modifier, and design and development of therapeutic antibodies.

### Module 4: What efficacy and safety profiles can we expect?
MoA inference for safety and efficacy profiles of drug candidates. The goal is to explore the difference between causal inference and statistical modelling, as well as the computational analysis and impact of single-cell omics data.

### Module 5: For which patients willl the drug work and how does it work, really?
Consider entry-into human and clinical studies from the perspective of PK/PD modelling, biomarker and causal inference.
