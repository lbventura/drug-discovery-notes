# Applied Mathematics and Informatics in Drug Discovery (University of Basel)

## Syllabus

See course notes https://accio.github.io/AMIDD/

### Lecture 1: Introduction to drug discovery

### Lecture 2: The What, the Who and the How of drug discovery

The workflow of modern drug discovery, the relevant stakeholders and the possible paths towards new drugs.

### Lecture 3: Key questions in drug discovery

Explore the 5 key questions in drug discovery:
- medical need,
- target and modality,
- PK/PD,
- benefit and risk,
- patient stratification,

### Lecture 4: Biological foundation of drug discovery

### Lecture 5: Protein as a drug target

Explore properties of proteins as drug targets, and learn an example of physics-based/mechanistic mathematical modelling.

### Lecture 6: Statistical model and machine learning

### Lecture 7: Causal inference (I)

Explore the concept of causality and application in data analysis.

### Lecture 8: Causal inference (II)

Demonstrate the difference between correlation and causality with real-world examples, and introduce techniques to infer causality.

### Lecture 9: Lead identification and optimization

High-level overview of the process of lead identification and optmization (LI/LO) of small-molecule drug discovery.

### Lecture 10: Mechanism and mode of action of drug candidates

### Lecture 11: PK/PD modeling and basics of clinical trials
