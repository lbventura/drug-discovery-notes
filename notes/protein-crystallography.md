# Protein Crystallography

It is not possible to analyze a protein with X-ray directly. This is because of:

- Weak signal: the scattering cross-section of carbon, nitrogen and oxygen is small,
- Radiation damage,

The solution is therefore to form a crystal containing 10^{13} - 10^{15} proteins. The incident radiation then undergoes constructive interference, amplifying the signal. Because the integrated intensity is proportional to the number of proteins in the crystal, the signal enhancement is of the order of 10^{15}.
